Chairman

Dr. Brijmohan Lall Munjal

Founder & Chairman, Hero Group

A visionary industrialist and philanthropist known for building one of India’s most resilient business legacies.

His leadership philosophy combines enterprise, nation-building, and people-first values that continue to define the group’s culture.

Dr. Brijmohan Lall Munjal is widely regarded as one of India’s most influential industrial leaders. He built Hero Group into a prominent global manufacturing and mobility enterprise through disciplined execution, innovation, and a deep belief in the power of people.

Under his guidance, the organization expanded across manufacturing, mobility, and social impact. His focus on operational excellence, strategic foresight, and long-term value creation helped the company navigate rapid growth and changing market dynamics.

Beyond business, he has been deeply committed to nation-building and philanthropy. His leadership reflects an enduring emphasis on education, enterprise, and community upliftment, leaving a legacy that extends well beyond corporate success.
